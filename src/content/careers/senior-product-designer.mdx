## About

GeoKade turns fragmented geospatial data into something municipalities and enterprises can actually use. You would own the surfaces where discovery becomes a map, and a map becomes a decision.

## What you'd do

- Design catalog search, layer inspection, and map storytelling end to end.
- Keep density honest: enough attribute detail for analysts, enough clarity for non-GIS users.
- Partner with engineering on Cloud (geokade.nl) and the corporate narrative on geokade.com.
- Run sessions with real users in government and infrastructure — not only prototype reviews.
- Hold the visual system: restrained type, orange accent, map-first composition.

## What we're looking for

- Five or more years designing products people use for work.
- Experience with maps, data-dense UIs, or complex B2B tools.
- Comfortable in the browser — you can ship a CSS or component fix without waiting.
- Strong opinions on hierarchy and restraint; you resist dashboard sprawl.
- You like talking to customers more than talking about frameworks.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
You've designed for GIS-adjacent tools, open data portals, or regulated public-sector software.
</Callout>
