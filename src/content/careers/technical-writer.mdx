## About

Geospatial products fail when docs assume everyone is a GIS specialist. You would own clear English (and growing Dutch) documentation for Cloud, Enterprise paths, and the changelog.

## What you'd do

- Write and structure product docs, how-tos, and integration notes.
- Keep the changelog honest: what shipped, what is pilot, what is not ready.
- Partner with design and engineering so UI copy and docs stay consistent.
- Turn customer questions into durable articles instead of one-off replies.

## What we're looking for

- Three or more years writing for developer or data products.
- You can explain CRS, layers, and sharing without drowning readers.
- Strong editing judgement; short sentences beat clever ones.
- Comfortable in Git-based docs workflows (Markdown/MDX).

<Callout title="Nice to have">
Dutch writing ability; experience with open-data or public-sector documentation.
</Callout>
