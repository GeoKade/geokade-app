## About

Spatial platforms live or die on projection correctness, tile performance, and boring reliability. You would make GeoKade's data plane trustworthy for Dutch open data and enterprise layers.

## What you'd do

- Build and maintain pipelines that ingest, normalise, and publish geospatial datasets.
- Own tiling, CRS handling, and serving strategies for interactive maps.
- Design retries, monitoring, and recovery for long-running spatial jobs.
- Partner with product on what "good enough for a municipal demo" vs "production SLA" means.

## What we're looking for

- Four or more years building production data or geospatial systems.
- Experience with PostGIS, GDAL/OGR, vector tiles, or cloud object storage for geo.
- You care about logs, tests, and reproducible CRS behaviour.
- Comfortable explaining trade-offs to non-GIS stakeholders.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
PDOK, BAG, BGT, or other Dutch open-data programmes; OGC API Features / WMS / WMTS.
</Callout>
