## About

Adoption fails quietly when users cannot find the right layer or share a map story. You would be the human bridge between GeoKade Cloud and the teams depending on it.

## What you'd do

- Onboard new organisations and guide first successful map workflows.
- Triage support, reproduce issues, and escalate with clear spatial context.
- Spot product gaps from tickets and feed them into the roadmap with evidence.
- Help customers move from curiosity to departmental habit — without overselling Intelligence.

## What we're looking for

- Experience in customer success, support, or GIS helpdesk for professional tools.
- Patient communicator; you can teach a non-GIS user and still satisfy a technical analyst.
- Dutch and English working proficiency preferred.
- Organised under concurrent threads; you document what you learn.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
Familiarity with municipal processes or utility operations.
</Callout>
