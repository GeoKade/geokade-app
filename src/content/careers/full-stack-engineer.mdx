## About

You would build the GeoKade Cloud experience founders and municipalities live in — from workspace auth to dataset APIs to the UI that makes layers understandable.

## What you'd do

- Ship features across the web app and API for discovery, maps, and collaboration.
- Own pieces of identity, workspaces, and sharing flows.
- Integrate open-data sources and internal datasets with clear contracts.
- Keep performance honest on large vector and raster workloads where it matters in the UI.

## What we're looking for

- Three or more years shipping full-stack product.
- Comfortable with TypeScript, React (or similar), and SQL or document stores.
- Interest in maps, geospatial formats, or data platforms is a plus — curiosity required either way.
- You enjoy talking to the people who use what you build.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
You've worked with MapLibre, PostGIS, OGC APIs, or public-sector open data.
</Callout>
