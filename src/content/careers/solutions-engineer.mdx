## About

Public-sector and infrastructure buyers need to see their territory in the product — not a generic SaaS tour. You would turn spatial questions into credible pilots and Enterprise paths.

## What you'd do

- Run discovery workshops and demos for municipalities, utilities, and enterprises.
- Shape pilot scopes that fit Cloud today and Enterprise controls where required.
- Translate customer requirements into crisp tickets for product and engineering.
- Support RFP responses with honest capability language — no invented certifications.

## What we're looking for

- Experience in solutions, pre-sales, or GIS consulting with European organisations.
- Comfortable on a map and in a spreadsheet; you can explain layers without jargon walls.
- Dutch and English working proficiency.
- You write clearly and prefer under-promising over theatrical roadmaps.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
Prior work with Dutch government ICT procurement or utility asset data.
</Callout>
