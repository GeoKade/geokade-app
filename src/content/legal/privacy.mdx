## Who this covers

This policy covers personal data we handle through geokade.nl (the corporate website), related marketing and contact channels, and — where applicable — GeoKade Cloud at geokade.nl and Enterprise deployments we operate or support.

It applies to website visitors, people who contact us, applicants, trial and workspace users, and invited organisation members.

Two roles matter for product use. For account, billing, and marketing data, GeoKade (Netherlands) is the controller. For content and datasets you load into a workspace — including organisational geospatial data — your organisation is typically the controller and we act as processor under your instructions and agreement.

## Where data comes from

Most of it you give us: contact forms, emails, account details, support tickets, and documents you upload. Some is generated as you use the services — usage logs, map session metadata, device and security telemetry. Some arrives from identity providers you connect (SSO) or from open-data sources you choose to query; those sources remain governed by their own terms.

## Product context and AI features

Where Intelligence or AI-assisted features are enabled, prompts and relevant spatial context may be sent to subprocessors under contractual terms. We design those flows to use the narrowest context needed for the request. Workspace content is not used to train public foundation models. Aggregated, de-identified signals (for example feature success rates) may be used to improve reliability.

Enterprise customers may negotiate stricter data-handling terms, including regional hosting preferences where offered.

<Callout title="How to control this">
Organisation admins can review connected identity, retention options, and feature flags with their GeoKade contact. Marketing email can be unsubscribed at any time.
</Callout>

## Why we process it

To respond to enquiries, operate Cloud and Enterprise services, authenticate users, provide support, bill contracted customers, detect abuse, comply with law, and improve the product. Where a legal basis is required, we typically rely on contract performance, legitimate interests (security and product improvement), consent for non-essential marketing cookies and email, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records.

## Who we share it with

Subprocessors bound by written terms who may only act on our instructions (hosting, email, analytics, identity, model providers where used). Authorities only where legally compelled; we notify you first unless prohibited. If the business is acquired, data transfers with it and this policy continues until replaced with notice. We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

Marketing and contact records are kept while relevant to the relationship, then deleted or anonymised. Workspace content stays while the account is active; after closure we provide a reasonable export window, then delete from live systems and, within a further period, from backups — except records we must keep (invoices, security logs required by law). Exact windows are stated in your order form or DPA where applicable.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Production systems use access control, logging, and least-privilege practices. If a breach affects your personal data, we notify affected parties without undue delay and within timelines required by applicable law (including GDPR where it applies).

## International transfers

GeoKade is based in the Netherlands. Infrastructure may use EU regions by default for Cloud; transfers outside the EEA, where they occur, rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Enterprise agreements can specify stronger residency requirements where available.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may request access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, restriction of processing, or objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time. We verify requests before acting. If you are an end user of a customer organisation, contact that organisation first — they control workspace content.

## Cookies and analytics

The corporate site may use strictly necessary cookies and, with consent where required, first-party analytics. The product app uses session and security cookies required to operate. We do not run advertising pixels on authenticated product pages.

## Children

GeoKade services are directed at professional and organisational use and are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We update this policy as services change. Material changes affecting customers are communicated with reasonable notice. The effective date at the top reflects the current version.
