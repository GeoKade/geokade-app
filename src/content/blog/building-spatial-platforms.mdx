Spatial products fail quietly when they get CRS wrong, when tiles lag, or when users cannot tell where a layer came from. Trust is an engineering property.

## Foundations we obsess over

1. **Provenance** — every layer should show source, update rhythm, and license context where relevant
2. **Performance** — interactive maps have to feel immediate on municipal hardware, not only on a developer laptop
3. **Permissions** — public open data and private enterprise layers must coexist without leaking scope
4. **Interoperability** — sit beside existing GIS stacks; do not force a rip-and-replace

These topics rarely appear in marketing pages. They decide whether a pilot becomes a platform.
