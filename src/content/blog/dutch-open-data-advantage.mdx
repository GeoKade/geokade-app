The Netherlands is an unusually strong place to build a geospatial intelligence company. Not because the market is large, but because the **data foundation** is deep, structured, and publicly available.

## What PDOK and BAG unlock

Services such as PDOK and the BAG give teams consistent access to buildings, addresses, administrative boundaries, and many other national layers. That lowers the cost of a credible first product — and raises the bar for quality.

For municipalities and infrastructure organisations, these sources are already familiar. The opportunity is not another download portal. It is a **workflow** that makes those datasets usable for decisions across departments.

## From NL to Europe

Our initial commercial focus is the Netherlands. The brand and platform are designed as a European technology vendor: international English on geokade.com, Dutch-first product experience on geokade.nl, and architecture that can grow beyond a single country.

Open data is the on-ramp. Enterprise data, private layers, and governance are the long game.
