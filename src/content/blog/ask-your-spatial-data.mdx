“Ask your spatial data” is a powerful idea — and an easy one to oversell. A general-purpose LLM can *talk* about maps. It cannot invent reliable geometry, topology, or institutional definitions.

## Grounded questions

GeoKade Intelligence is the capability layer where supported questions become:

- data queries against known sources
- spatial operations (buffer, intersect, filter, aggregate)
- visual results on the map
- explanations that cite the layers used

Examples of the kind of questions we care about:

1. Which buildings fall inside this flood-risk zone?
2. Where are suitable sites within walking distance of public facilities?
3. Which neighbourhoods show the highest density for this indicator?

## Honesty about the roadmap

Some of this is available or emerging in Cloud. Some of it is roadmap. We mark the difference clearly — because trust is part of the product for public-sector and enterprise buyers.
