GIS experts can live inside layers and attribute tables. Policy makers, planners, and executives usually cannot. They need a **narrative** with a map — not a dump of every available layer.

## What a good map story does

- opens with the decision question
- shows only the layers that matter
- explains uncertainty and source quality
- ends with a clear next step

GeoKade treats stories as first-class artefacts: create, share, and revisit them as the underlying data updates.

## Design principle

Geographic visuals should support understanding. Contours, grids, and layer stacks are welcome when they clarify space — not when they decorate it.
