Municipalities, utilities, and infrastructure organisations sit on rich geographic data: buildings, parcels, networks, environmental layers, mobility, and energy. Much of it is public. Much of it is locked inside specialist tools.

GeoKade exists to close that gap — **discover, map, analyze, understand** — in one environment that non-GIS specialists can use, while remaining useful for analysts.

## What “actionable” actually means

A map is not the finish line. Actionable intelligence means a stakeholder can answer a concrete question:

1. Where should we prioritise inspection this quarter?
2. Which assets sit inside a risk zone?
3. What changed in this neighbourhood over the last five years?

Those answers need authoritative layers, clear provenance, and a workflow teams can share.

## Cloud, Enterprise, Intelligence

We describe three complementary streams:

- **GeoKade Cloud** — hosted discovery, maps, and collaboration on [geokade.nl](https://geokade.nl)
- **GeoKade Enterprise** — private deployment, governance, and integration
- **GeoKade Intelligence** — spatial questions grounded in real datasets

We do not advertise unfinished capabilities as production features. Roadmap items stay labelled as roadmap.
